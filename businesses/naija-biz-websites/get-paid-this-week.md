# NaijaBiz Sites — Get Paid This Week

Goal: **first paid website ($150+) within 7–10 days.**

## 1. Payment setup (one-time — shared with Fixit IT Support)

Use the same accounts as `../diaspora-it-support/get-paid-this-week.md`:
- **PayPal.Me link**: for diaspora clients paying in USD/GBP
- **Wise**: USD/GBP details for bigger payments (cheaper fees)
- **For clients inside Nigeria:** Nigerian clients often can't pay with PayPal. Ask a trusted family member's NGN account to receive payment, or use **Wise → NGN** / **Grey** / **Chipper Cash**. Agree on the exchange rate in writing before the work starts.

**Payment rule: 50% deposit before you start, 50% before the site goes live.** Never hand over the files or domain until you've been paid in full.

## 2. Packages

| Package | Price | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Landing Page (1 page + WhatsApp button) | $150 | 3 days |
| Business Site (5 pages + Google Maps + contact form) | $250 | 7 days |
| E-Commerce (product catalog + WhatsApp/Paystack checkout) | $400 | 14 days |
| Maintenance plan | $15–30/mo | ongoing |

Hosting on Vercel/Netlify is free. The client pays for their own domain (~$10–15/yr) and owns it.

## 3. Who to target (best → hardest)

1. **Diaspora-owned businesses**: Nigerian restaurants, shops, salons, churches, and cargo/shipping companies in Japan, UK, US, and Canada. These are the easiest because they can pay in USD.
2. **Family and friends' businesses in Nigeria**: they trust you, and their sites become your first portfolio pieces.
3. **Nigerian businesses that only have Instagram**: find them on Instagram/Google Maps with no website listed.

**Daily target:** 10 personal DMs + 1 group post.

## 4. Portfolio trick (no clients yet?)

Build **1 free demo site** for a real business you know (a relative's shop, your church). Use it as your example in every message: "Here's one I built: [link]". One real example sells better than any description.
